The idea

A sentence is finished when it gets its period. So is a task.

Every item in Done starts as an open circle. As the work happens, the circle fills. When it’s finished, it closes into a period. That’s the whole interface. Everything else stays out of your way.

01

One shape. Five meanings.

You never have to read a status. You just glance.

  • Waiting Written down. It’ll start soon, or when you said.
  • On it The ring is how far along. The pulse means it’s alive.
  • Needs you The only colour that asks for attention. It’s rare on purpose.
  • Hit a snag Something didn’t work. It’s already trying another way.
  • Done. Closed into a period. With a receipt, so you can check.
02

Underneath every sentence is the full record of what happened. You only see it if you ask.

04

When something breaks, it tells you. Then it fixes it.

Websites go down. Forms change. Passwords expire. Done doesn’t pretend. It says what went wrong in one line, tries another way, and only asks you if it runs out of good options.

  1. Signing in to your gym account.

  2. The cancel button is hidden for online accounts. Trying another way.

  3. Their terms say a written request works. Drafted one for you to approve.

  4. Cancelled. Confirmation saved. You’ll save $39 a month.
